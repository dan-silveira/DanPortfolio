Digital Passport Application | 2023

Sector: Government

Leadership | Design System | Prototyping

The Government of Canada developed an entirely digital passport renewal experience as application volumes increased year over year and impacted wait times at service centres. As design lead and AI specialist, I delivered the design system and designed the end-to-end renewal platform, including leading the design of the AI-powered computer vision tool that evaluates passport photos in real time. The digital application enabled citizens to renew passports from home while reducing pressure on physical service centres.
